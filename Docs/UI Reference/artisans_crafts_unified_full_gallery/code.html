Bamboo & Cane

Mastering the Elements

From the dense forests to the potter's wheel, artisans in Meghalaya utilize the earth's provisions with profound skill. The intricate geometry of bamboo weaving and the raw, fired finish of Sung Valley clay speak to a deep, sustainable connection with the environment.

The Cycle of Creation

1

Harvesting

Foraging and ethically harvesting natural materials like Eri silk cocoons and mature bamboo.

2

Preparation

Spinning threads, splitting cane, or kneading clay into workable forms by hand.

3

Crafting

The meditative process of weaving, shaping, or molding the materials into intricate designs.

4

Finishing

Dyeing with natural colors, firing clay, or adding final structural touches to complete the piece.

Securing the Future

TRIS is committed to ensuring that the ancestral wisdom of Meghalaya's artisans is not lost to time. Through our preservation initiatives, we create sustainable pathways for younger generations to inherit these invaluable skills.

  • school
    Apprenticeship Programs Connecting young learners with master artisans for hands-on training.
  • eco
    Sustainable Sourcing Funding community forests to protect raw materials for future use.
